# About Me

Welcome to my digital garden — a space where ideas take root and grow.

I'm an e-commerce strategist by day, working with global teams to help brands connect with their customers in meaningful ways. My career has taken me across continents, from India to the United States, giving me a unique perspective on how technology shapes human connection across cultures.

But work is just one part of who I am. When I'm not in meetings or diving into data, you'll find me exploring the night sky with my telescope, camera in hand, trying to capture the cosmos. There's something humbling about photographing distant galaxies — it reminds me that we're all part of something much larger.

I've recently fallen in love with what I call "vibe coding" — using AI as a creative partner to build tools and experiments. It's less about perfect code and more about bringing ideas to life quickly, learning in public, and sharing the journey.

This site is my corner of the internet. It's where I share what I'm building, what I'm learning, and what catches my curiosity. Think of it as a living document — always evolving, never quite finished.
